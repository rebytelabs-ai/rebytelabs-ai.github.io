2. The App

FlipFocus is a Pomodoro timer presented as a retro mechanical flip clock. It offers cycles of 25/5, 50/10 and 90/15 minutes, focus tags, statistics displayed on graph paper, and visual and sound themes.

The timer is based on the end time you set, so it keeps running when the App is closed or the screen is locked. A notification is sent at the end of each cycle, provided that notifications are allowed on your device.
