Frequently asked questions

What is FlipFocus?

FlipFocus is a Pomodoro timer that shows time on a retro mechanical flip clock inspired by the 1970s. Each digit flips with a 3D animation, a click sound, and a light vibration.

Which focus cycles can I use?

You can choose 25/5, 50/10, or 90/15 minute cycles. Advanced features also let you set custom cycle lengths.

Does the timer keep running when the app is closed or the screen is locked?

Yes. The timer is based on the end time, so it keeps running when you close the app or lock the screen. A notification alerts you when the cycle ends.

Can I tag my focus sessions?

Yes. You can link each session to a built-in focus tag or to a tag you create yourself.

Where can I see my progress?

Your sessions are shown on a graph paper style statistics view, so you can see your focus sessions at a glance.

Does the app need an account?

No. FlipFocus does not require you to create an account.

Purchases and restoring

FlipFocus uses a freemium model. Some advanced features, such as custom cycle durations and ambient sounds, are unlocked with a one-time purchase.

To restore a purchase, open the app and use the Restore button while signed in to the same store account you used to buy it.

Purchases are processed by a third-party payment provider. For payment questions, you may also contact your store account provider.

Data we process

Our developer disclosures state that purchase and usage data are collected and processed on servers. We do not use advertising or tracking.

Purchase data is used to confirm your one-time purchase and unlock features. Usage data is used to operate and improve the app.

Payments are handled by a third-party payment provider. We do not sell your data.

The app does not offer an in-app option to delete an account. To request deletion of your data, email us at rebytelabs@gmail.com.

Our privacy policy explains the legal basis for processing and how long data is kept. Please read it at privacy.html.
