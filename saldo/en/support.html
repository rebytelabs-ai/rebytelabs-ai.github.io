Support – Saldo

About Saldo

Saldo is a personal budget app for iOS and Android. It helps you track your income and expenses, and manage your budget day to day.

Your financial entries stay on your device. Saldo does not send your transactions to our servers, so your finances remain confidential.

The app is rated 4+, contains no ads, and does not track you. Saldo is developed by Rebyte Labs.

Contact us

For help, questions or bug reports, email us at rebytelabs@gmail.com.

We reply within 2 business days. Please include your device model, operating system version and a short description of the issue so we can help you faster.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Tap the “Restore” button in the app and sign in with the same store account you used for the original purchase.

Is there a limit on how many transactions I can enter?

No. Saldo lets you enter all your transactions without restriction.

Can I back up my data?

Yes. You can export your data as a CSV file to save it or share it. Exported files are created on your device.

Does Saldo work without an internet connection?

Your budget data is stored on your device. For purchases, an internet connection is needed to complete the transaction with the store.

Your data and privacy

Your budget data (transactions, income, expenses and categories) is stored only on your device.

Purchase-related data is collected on our servers. Payments are handled by third-party payment providers.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, correct, and request erasure of your personal data. You may also request restriction of processing, object to processing, and request data portability.

The legal basis for processing purchase-related data is the performance of the purchase contract and our legitimate interest in managing purchases.

We keep purchase-related data only as long as needed for these purposes and to meet legal obligations.

To exercise your rights, email rebytelabs@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

Purchases and payments

Saldo is free to download and offers a one-time in-app purchase.

Payments are processed by third-party payment providers. Purchases are managed through your App Store or Google Play account.

If you have a billing issue, contact the store that processed your payment, or email us at rebytelabs@gmail.com.

Account deletion

Saldo does not offer an in-app account deletion option.

To request deletion of purchase-related personal data, email rebytelabs@gmail.com.
